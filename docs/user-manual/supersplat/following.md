title: Following Creators
description: "Follow creators on superspl.at and see their latest splats in your Following feed."
---

Found a creator whose work you love? Follow them, and their splats show up in your **Following** feed on the [superspl.at](https://superspl.at) home page.

## Follow a creator

1. Open the creator's [profile](/user-manual/supersplat/user-profile), or any of their [scenes](/user-manual/supersplat/scene-page).
2. Click **Follow**.

The button changes to **Following**. You'll need to be signed in with your PlayCanvas account.

## See splats from people you follow {#following-feed}

On the [home page](https://superspl.at), open the **Following** tab. It shows the public splats of everyone you follow, newest first, including the ones they published before you followed them.

## Find more creators {#lists}

Every profile shows how many followers a creator has and how many people they follow. Click either number to see the list, and click anyone in it to visit their profile. Seeing who your favorite creators follow is a great way to discover new ones.

## Unfollow a creator {#managing}

Click **Following** on the creator's profile or on one of their scenes.

To review everyone you follow in one place, open your own profile and click your **following** count. Click **Following** next to anyone you want to unfollow.
