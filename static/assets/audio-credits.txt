Audio for the live examples in the Audio section of the PlayCanvas User Manual
==============================================================================

bell.mp3, sfx.mp3 and music.mp3 were generated for these examples with ElevenLabs
(https://elevenlabs.io) on 2026-11-16, then trimmed, normalized and encoded with ffmpeg.

bell.mp3
    Tool:    ElevenLabs Sound Effects (eleven_text_to_sound_v2)
    Prompt:  "A single clear ding of a small brass reception desk call bell, struck once,
             dry and close, no reverb, no background noise"
    Changes: mono, trimmed to 1.4 s with a fade out, normalized, 96 kbps MP3.

sfx.mp3
    An audio sprite of four sounds, each generated on its own and laid end to end
    with silence between them.
    Tool:    ElevenLabs Sound Effects (eleven_text_to_sound_v2)
    Sounds:  coin       0.0 s, 0.7 s long  "Retro video game coin pickup, a bright two-note chime, short and clean"
             jump       1.0 s, 0.8 s long  "Retro video game jump sound, a quick rising synth whoop, short and clean"
             laser      2.0 s, 0.7 s long  "Sci-fi laser blaster firing a single shot, a short pew, clean"
             explosion  3.0 s, 1.3 s long  "Short punchy retro video game explosion, a low boom with a brief noise tail"
    Changes: mono, each sound trimmed, faded out and normalized, 96 kbps MP3.

music.mp3
    Tool:    ElevenLabs Music (music_v2_5), instrumental
    Prompt:  "Bright, relaxed instrumental background music for a casual puzzle video game.
             Marimba, soft synth pads and light percussion at a steady 100 BPM. Cheerful and
             unobtrusive, with constant energy throughout and no intro or outro."
    Changes: the first 8 bars (19.2 s at 100 BPM) cut into a seamless loop, with the
             0.6 s after the loop crossfaded into its start, 128 kbps stereo MP3.

footsteps.mp3 and drop.mp3 come from the PlayCanvas engine and web-components examples.
