Color and Appearance

Two different sets of controls affect how a splat looks in SuperSplat:

  • The Colors section of the Scene Manager changes the splat data itself: tint, temperature, saturation, brightness, tonal range, and transparency. These edits are saved and exported with the splat.
  • The mode strip and the viewport colors in Preferences change only how the viewport draws the markers and the selection. They never modify the splat.

Colors​

Expand the Colors section at the bottom of the Scene Manager, below Transform, by clicking its header. The sliders hold a pending color grade that is previewed live in the viewport on the Gaussians it would affect: the current selection, or the whole active splat when nothing is selected. Click Apply to bake the grade into those Gaussians; the sliders then return to neutral, ready for the next adjustment. To grade one region differently from the rest, select it first.

Controls​

ControlRangeEffect
TintcolorMultiplies the color by the chosen tint. White leaves the source color unchanged.
Temperature−0.5 to 0.5Shifts the result toward cooler (negative) or warmer (positive) tones.
Saturation0 to 2Reduces or increases color intensity. 1 leaves saturation unchanged.
Brightness−1 to 1Darkens or brightens. 0 leaves brightness unchanged.
Black Point0 to 1Raises the darkest value that is mapped to black, increasing shadow clipping.
White Point0 to 1Lowers the brightest value that is mapped to white, increasing highlight clipping.
Transparency−6 to 6Reduces (negative) or increases (positive) opacity relative to the source values.

Black Point always stays below White Point: moving one into the other pushes the other along. Reset removes any grade already applied to the targeted Gaussians and returns the sliders to neutral. Changing the selection discards a pending grade that has not been applied. Apply and Reset are recorded in the edit history and can be undone and redone.

A Practical Correction Workflow​

  1. Set the viewport tone mapping and background color in Preferences, so you are judging the scene in its intended viewing conditions.
  2. Correct overall warmth with Temperature.
  3. Adjust Brightness, then set Black Point and White Point to establish the tonal range.
  4. Use Saturation and Tint for the final color balance.
  5. Check the result from several camera angles. Spherical harmonic color can change with view direction.
  6. Click Apply.

Tone mapping and the background color are view settings; the Colors section changes the splat itself.

Copy and Paste a Grade​

Right-click the Colors header and choose Copy to copy every slider value, or right-click a single row to copy just that value. The values are copied as plain JSON text, for example {"tint":[1,1,1],"temperature":0.1,"saturation":1.2,"brightness":0,"blackPoint":0,"whitePoint":1,"transparency":0}, so you can keep them in a note or paste them into another Editor session. Paste on the header accepts any subset of these keys, so {"saturation":1.2} changes only saturation; Paste on a row accepts a bare value or a copied section. Tint also accepts a hex color such as #ffcc88. Pasted values are clamped to each slider's range, and invalid clipboard contents change nothing and briefly flash the target red.

Copy and paste work on the pending grade shown in the sliders: click Apply after pasting to bake it in.

Save and Export Behavior​

Applied grades become part of the splat's color data, so they are stored in .ssproj projects and included in every export format. Click Apply before saving or exporting: a pending grade that has not been applied is only a preview.

Markers and Selection Display​

How the viewport draws markers and the selection is set on the mode strip beside the View Cube. These settings are saved with your preferences and affect only the viewport. See Selection Mode and X-Ray for how the mode and X-Ray change what the selection tools hit.

Mode Options​

Press and hold Centers or Rings on the mode strip to open its options. A short press just chooses the mode.

ModeControlDefaultDescription
CentersCenter Size2Size of the center dots in pixels (0–10).
Splat ColorsOffDraw unselected center dots in the colors of their Gaussians instead of the unselected color.
RingsRing Thickness4Thickness of the ring outlines (1–50).
Splat ColorsOnDraw unselected rings in the colors of their Gaussians instead of the unselected color.

Selected markers always use the selected color.

Markers Options​

Press and hold Markers on the mode strip to open its options. A short press toggles the markers, like Tab.

ControlDefaultDescription
Markers OnlyOffHide the Gaussians themselves while the markers are shown, leaving only the dots or rings.
Tint SelectionOffTint the selected Gaussians with the selected color.
Outline SelectionOffDraw an outline around the silhouette of the selection in the selected color.

Viewport Colors​

The Background Color, Selected Color, Unselected Color, and Locked Color are set in the Colors section of the Preferences popup. They are used by the markers, the selection tint and outline, and the Splat Data histogram.

For per-Gaussian inspection and selection by color or other attributes, use the Splat Data panel. To animate the viewport camera after finishing the splat's appearance, see the Timeline.
