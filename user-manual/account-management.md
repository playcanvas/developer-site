# Account Management

Your PlayCanvas account signs you in to the PlayCanvas Editor and SuperSplat. This section covers creating an account and the settings that go with it, organizations for teams, and billing.

## Why Create an Account?

A free PlayCanvas account lets you use the [PlayCanvas Editor](https://developer.playcanvas.com/user-manual/editor.md) to build 3D apps with a visual, collaborative interface. Even if you work with the [PlayCanvas Engine](https://developer.playcanvas.com/user-manual/engine.md), [PlayCanvas Web Components](https://developer.playcanvas.com/user-manual/web-components.md) or [PlayCanvas React](https://developer.playcanvas.com/user-manual/react.md) instead, an account lets you:

- **Explore and learn** - Browse the scripts and assets of thousands of public Editor projects for ideas and inspiration.
- **Generate assets** - Use the Editor's asset processing to prepare assets for any PlayCanvas-based project.
- **Publish Gaussian splats** - Publish splats to [SuperSplat](https://developer.playcanvas.com/user-manual/supersplat.md), and like and comment on other people's. Browsing the public [Explore](https://developer.playcanvas.com/user-manual/supersplat/viewing/explore.md) gallery and viewing public scene pages doesn't need an account.
- **Join the community** - Star, watch and comment on other people's projects.
- **Stay informed** - Hear about updates across the PlayCanvas platform.
