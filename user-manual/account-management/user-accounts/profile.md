# Your Profile

Every PlayCanvas account has a public profile. Its address is:

```none
https://playcanvas.com/user/<USERNAME>
```

To open your own, select your name at the bottom of the [Project Dashboard](https://developer.playcanvas.com/user-manual/editor/projects/dashboard.md) sidebar and choose **View public profile**.

[Image: Your Profile]

Your profile shows:

- Your picture, full name and username
- When you joined PlayCanvas
- Your skills, such as **Coder** or **Artist**
- The total **views**, **plays** and **stars** of your public projects
- Your **Public projects**

Only your public projects are listed, so anyone can view your profile, including visitors who aren't logged in. When you view your own profile, a note at the top reminds you that this is how others see it.

## Public Projects

Search your public projects by name, owner or description, or narrow the list with the same filters as the [Project Dashboard](https://developer.playcanvas.com/user-manual/editor/projects/dashboard.md#finding-projects), such as `name:`. **Sort by** orders the projects by **Last edited**, **Last opened**, **Created**, **Name**, **Most viewed**, **Most played** or **Size**, **Descending** or **Ascending**. The buttons next to it switch between cards and a list.

## Editing Your Profile

Select **Edit profile** to open your [Account Settings](https://developer.playcanvas.com/user-manual/account-management/user-accounts/settings.md) in a new tab. There you can change your picture, username, full name and skills, and add links to your website and social accounts.

:::tip

Once you're logged in, [playcanvas.com](https://playcanvas.com) takes you to your [Project Dashboard](https://developer.playcanvas.com/user-manual/editor/projects/dashboard.md), which lists all of your projects, public and private.

:::
